    Data provenance & limitations

    Historical strike and takedown accuracy values are supplied by the source. Their exact collection timestamps cannot be independently certified. The report includes a model without these statistics.

    No guarantees for unseen athletes, new divisions, cross-division fights, or future UFC events. Injuries, training camps and tactical changes are not observed. Draws and no contests are outside the binary task.

    Educational sports analytics prototype. Estimates are not verified betting advice or causal explanations.
